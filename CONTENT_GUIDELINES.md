# 📋 Content Guidelines for LearnSmart LMS

Maintain professional, LMS-specific content throughout the platform.

---

## ✅ DO's

### Content
- ✅ Use real course/learning-related topics
- ✅ Include specific technologies and skills (React, Python, etc.)
- ✅ Mention benefits to learners (career advancement, certificates, etc.)
- ✅ Reference actual features (lifetime access, instructor profiles, etc.)
- ✅ Use action-oriented language (Learn, Master, Build, etc.)

### Examples
- ✅ "Learn Full-Stack Web Development with React and Node.js"
- ✅ "Master digital marketing and grow your business online"
- ✅ "Build real projects and earn certificates"
- ✅ "Learn from industry experts"
- ✅ "Get lifetime access to all course materials"

### Professional Tone
- ✅ Professional but approachable
- ✅ Clear and concise
- ✅ Benefit-focused
- ✅ Student-centric
- ✅ Action-oriented CTAs

---

## ❌ DON'Ts

### Content to Avoid
- ❌ Lorem Ipsum or any placeholder Latin text
- ❌ Generic "Step 1, Step 2, Step 3..." descriptions
- ❌ "Example" or "Sample" content
- ❌ Filler text with no substance
- ❌ Off-topic or non-educational content
- ❌ Future dates (use current/past dates)
- ❌ Fake author names (use real or LearnSmart Team)

### Examples to Avoid
- ❌ "Nemo enim ipsam voluptatem quia..."
- ❌ "There are many variations of passages..."
- ❌ "Generic Step 1: Do Something"
- ❌ "Take a sample lesson"
- ❌ "Purchase the course"

## 📝 Content Categories

### 1. Learning Paths / Process Steps
**Length**: 1-2 sentences per step
**Focus**: Clear progression through learning journey
**Tone**: Encouraging and empowering

Example:
```
Step 01: Search & Browse Courses
Explore thousands of courses across various categories. Use filters to find 
exactly what you need.
```

### 2. Course Information
**Length**: 2-3 paragraphs
**Focus**: What platform offers, why users should join
**Tone**: Professional and benefits-focused

Example:
```
LearnSmart offers expertly-crafted courses designed to help you master 
in-demand skills. With interactive lessons, real-world projects, and lifetime 
access, you'll gain practical knowledge you can apply immediately.
```

### 3. Email Subscriptions
**Length**: 1-2 sentences
**Focus**: Value proposition, clear CTA
**Tone**: Engaging and action-oriented

Example:
```
Stay updated with the latest courses, exclusive workshops, and webinars. 
Get notified about industry trends and career advancement opportunities.
```

### 4. Blog Posts
**Length**: 50-150 words excerpt, full article
**Focus**: Educational, relevant to course topics
**Tone**: Informative and inspiring

Example:
```
Master modern web development with our comprehensive guide. Learn HTML, CSS, 
JavaScript, React, Node.js, and MongoDB. Build real-world projects and launch 
your career as a full-stack developer.
```

## 🔄 Content Update Process

When updating content:

1. **Review**: Check current content
2. **Identify**: Spot Lorem Ipsum or generic text
3. **Replace**: Add LMS-specific content
4. **Verify**: Check for professionalism
5. **Build**: Test with `npm run build`
6. **Review**: Visual inspection
7. **Commit**: Push changes to repository
